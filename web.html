Our Story

Welcome to Imperial Multi Cuisine Restaurant

Tucked into Saidabad Main Road in New Malakpet, Imperial brings together the slow-cooked heritage of Hyderabadi dum biryani with the everyday comforts of Indian, Chinese, Mughlai and Continental cooking — under one roof, at one table.

Every biryani is sealed and cooked on dum the traditional way; every gravy is finished to order. Whether it's a family Sunday lunch, a celebration, or a quick dinner run, we cook it like it's for our own family.
